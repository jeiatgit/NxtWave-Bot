What changed

First idea: IIT/NIT students, LinkedIn DMs and a small paid boost.

Final: Tier-2/3 students, class reps, a referral loop and no paid ads.

Why: the paid math couldn't reach 500, and this audience trusts peers.

What AI suggested and I rejected

  • A formal WhatsApp message: I made it short and conversational
